Privacy Policy: Dog Tower Defense

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Dog Tower Defense ("the game") is made by Jack Ermence ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

What we collect

We don't collect personal information. The game has no accounts or logins and no chat. Your progress (Treats, unlocked dogs, training, scores, settings, and any names you give your dogs) and the birth year you enter at the age check are saved only on your device. If you create a save code, it contains only your game progress, and you decide where to keep it. If iCloud save is on (Settings), a copy of your game progress is also stored in your own iCloud account through Apple, so it can sync to your other devices. We can't see or access it. You can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Third-party services

The game uses a few services that may process limited data on our behalf:

  • Google AdMob shows optional reward videos. If you are 13 or older, iOS asks you once (before your first reward video) whether to allow tracking. If you allow it, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads that fit your interests. If you don't (and always in kid-safe mode), ads are non-personalized. You see the same number of ads either way, and you can change your choice any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. AdMob may also collect device information (such as IP address and ad interaction data) to deliver ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat processes in-app purchases so they can be restored on your other devices. It receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipts from Apple. It also receives how much each optional reward video earned and where in the game it was shown, so we can see ad and purchase earnings together. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Expo Observe (game analytics) receives a few anonymous gameplay events, such as which levels were cleared or lost, how many days the game has been played, and whether kid-safe mode is on, plus app startup timing. It uses a random ID for each installation (it resets if the app is reinstalled) and never receives names, ages or advertising identifiers. We use it only to improve the game, never for ads. See https://expo.dev/privacy
  • Sentry (crash reports, if enabled) receives technical details when the game crashes, such as the error, device model and iOS version. It is set up not to collect IP addresses or personal data. See https://sentry.io/privacy/

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is made for a general audience. On first launch we ask for a birth year (kept only on the device). Players under 13 get kid-safe mode: ads are marked as child-directed under COPPA (no personalization, G-rated only), there are no limited-time offers, a grown-up check appears before any purchase or outside link, and dog names are picked from a preset list instead of typed. Everyone else gets family-friendly (PG) non-personalized ads.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all game data stored on your device. To ask about data held by Google, RevenueCat or Expo, contact us and we will help.
