The cats are coming, and only your pups can stop them!

Build a pack from 25 dog breeds, each with powers based on what the breed is really known for. Corgis launch tennis balls, Huskies howl, Dachshunds hunt the biggest bosses, and Great Danes shake the ground. Upgrade every dog, train them in the Kennel all the way to Legend, and unlock special abilities you trigger mid-battle.

  • 15 levels, from the backyard to the Cat Castle siege and out to the Cat Mothership in space
  • 50 waves per level, with mini-bosses, bosses and 30 unique level bosses
  • A free Weekly Boss Event with Bronze, Silver and Gold rewards
  • Easy, Medium, Hard, Endless mode and replay modifiers
  • Collect dog cards, rare dogs, 66 cute outfits and every cat in the Cat Collection
  • Train every breed to Kennel level 15 and earn the Legend rank
  • Visit the Dog Park to watch your pack play, pet your pups and play fetch
  • Daily and weekly challenges and a 28-day login calendar
  • Plays offline. Every dog can be unlocked by playing.
