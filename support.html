Support

Need help? Email eplussgaming@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within 2 business days. Please include your iPhone or iPad model and what happened.

Common questions

I bought something. How do I get it back on a new device?
Open the Shop (or Settings) and tap Restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.
How do I move my progress to a new iPhone or iPad?
Turn on iCloud save in Settings → Your save, and sign in with the same Apple ID on the new device. Without iCloud, use Back up save code on the old device and Load a save code on the new one.
How do I turn off purchases for my child?
Settings → Family and purchases → Turn off all purchases. Players under 13 also get kid-safe mode automatically, with a grown-up check before any purchase and dog names picked from a kid-friendly list. You can also use Screen Time on iPhone to block in-app purchases.
How do I get a refund?
Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
What are the odds in card packs?
The exact odds for every pack are shown in the game next to each pack before you open or buy it. A rare dog is guaranteed at least every 10 packs. Packs can always be earned free by playing.
The game feels slow on my device.
Settings → Graphics → Low turns off extra effects. Reduce Motion also turns off screen shake and flashes.
